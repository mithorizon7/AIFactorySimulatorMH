# Facilitator tasks and rubric — pilot-tasks-1

Collection disabled. Use only after the [activation gates](../pilot-operations.md)
are satisfied. Read quoted prompts exactly, without pointing to controls or
explaining a correct strategy. Keep the same assessment/task revisions through a
cohort. Participants may use normal accommodations; inaccessible controls are a
product finding, not a participant failure.

## Session sequence and clocks

After approved consent, administer pre-assessment without coaching. Use a fresh
disposable browser profile on the approved exact origin, never a personal
profile. Do not collect precise user-agent strings. Record broad browser/device,
input/accommodation category, deployed SHA/version tuple and observation clock.
One facilitator observes; no recordings. A wall-clock session timer measures
task experience; game active time is separate and excludes pause/closed windows.

After gameplay, administer the same post-assessment, then ask: "How enjoyable was
this, from 1 not enjoyable to 5 very enjoyable? You may skip." Finally ask neutrally:
"Is there anything else you would like to do?" Record spontaneous replay/request
before asking any direct replay question. Do not count compliance with T6 as
voluntary replay. Give answer feedback only after post-assessment is complete.

## Outcome coding, frozen before sessions

- I — Independent: task success without strategy/control-location hints. Reading
  the exact prompt again or arranging an agreed accommodation is not a hint;
  record it separately.
- P — Prompted: success after any control-location, strategy or interpretation
  hint. Record the exact hint in the restricted observation notes, never public
  issue text. Prompted success is not independent success.
- F — Failed: participant tries but cannot meet success before the task/session
  limit, chooses to stop that task, or reaches a soft lock. Record cause without
  blame. Voluntary withdrawal is recorded separately and is not silently F.
- A — Inaccessible: a product barrier blocks the participant's input or assistive
  technology. Record barrier and accommodation; do not pool it as lack of skill.
- N — Not attempted/not observed: exact reason required. Missing is not success.

At 90 seconds without T1 success, record threshold failure before offering one
neutral prompt: "What information on this screen might help?" At any later
120 seconds without a valid action, first record the state/blocker and whether a
legal enabled action actually exists. "No valid action for over 2 minutes" is
counted only after checking that claim, not from participant uncertainty alone.
Offer assistance only after recording the independent outcome and ask whether
the participant wants it. Any urgent distress/accessibility request overrides
timing; stop or accommodate, document privately, and never prioritize a metric.

## Defects and denominators

Save corruption: the last acknowledged valid save cannot be reopened or recovered
without losing it. Soft lock: no valid progress/recovery action is available and
reopening does not restore one. Preserve the synthetic reproduction separately;
do not upload participant snapshots. Escalate immediately to the owner, pause
new sessions for possible data loss, and record fix/affected retest evidence.

Report each task'sI/P/F/A/N counts and withdrawal/missingness. The frozen cohort
targets use all 15 consented sessions, not only completers. Also show relevant
paired/attempted denominators alongside them. If fewer than 15 are enrolled, show
n/actual and mark the 15-person gate unevaluated rather than rescaling it to pass.

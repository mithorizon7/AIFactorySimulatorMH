# Pilot participant notice — pilot-consent-1

Draft for research-owner review. Collection is disabled. Do not recruit or ask
anyone to sign this draft until the owner supplies an accessible contact channel,
approves the consent/guardian process and proves the separate study system and
retention/withdrawal controls. This is not legal or institutional approval.

## What you would be invited to do

You would try a fictional AI-business management game, answer the same five
short questions before and after, and tell a facilitator what is clear or
confusing. Allow up to 45 minutes, including up to 35 minutes of gameplay. You
could use your normal accessibility tools, ask for a break, skip any question or
stop. There is no competition or judgment of your intelligence. Model Capability
is a game measure; future GNT eras are speculative, not forecasts.

Participation is voluntary. Declining or stopping has no penalty. You may decline
observation or optional product analytics separately from playing the anonymous
game. No payment or incentive is promised by this draft. Any later incentive
needs an owner-approved notice before invitation.

## What would be collected, only after permission

The restricted study dataset would contain a random study identifier, task
outcomes/timings, assistance and accessibility observations, selected assessment
answers, aggregate correctness, and optional enjoyment/replay responses. The
facilitator must not record names, emails, unrelated personal stories, raw
browser history, IP addresses or credentials in the observation record.
No audio/video/screen recording is requested or authorized by this notice.

Consent/contact records would be held separately from responses. The app's
general analytics role must not be able to read raw study answers or consent.
Optional product analytics may contain bounded anonymous events and aggregate
assessment correctness, never raw answers or free text; opting out does not
prevent play. A synthetic canary or a successful technical test is not a study
participant.

Hosting/proxy access logs may contain IP addresses. The actual provider log
retention is not yet verified; the target is the shortest supported setting,
aiming for 7 days. Because that policy and the restricted collection system are
unresolved, study collection remains disabled. The final notice must state the
actual applicable policy before anyone is invited.

## Retention and withdrawal

Raw study responses would expire 180 days after collection, with verified deletion.
Approved deidentified aggregates may remain. General product events have a
separate 90-day maximum. See the [data procedure](data-boundaries-and-withdrawal.md)
for exceptions that must be resolved before activation, including backup expiry.

You would receive a random withdrawal code. Give only that code through the
owner-approved private contact route to request withdrawal. Do not post it in
GitHub or public chat. The custodian would remove linked responses, consent
mapping and identifiable observations, verify removal, and tell you about any
bounded backup expiry. Once data has been irreversibly deidentified into an
aggregate, it cannot be linked back to your code for removal. This limit must be
explained before consent, not introduced after a request.

Owner/contact: **not yet assigned — recruitment blocker**.
Custodian/private withdrawal route: **not yet approved — collection blocker**.
Applicable provider/backup deletion limits: **unverified — collection blocker**.

## Permission record, outside this repository

The owner-approved restricted system must record the notice revision, affirmative
permission, date, optional analytics choice, withdrawal-code receipt and
facilitator identifier. Do not fill participant names or signatures into this
file. Permission must be understandable, unpressured and accessible.

Audience includes ages 14–adult. For anyone under 18, the research owner must first
approve the applicable guardian-permission and young-person-assent process;
guardian permission alone does not override a young person's refusal. Do not
collect a birth date merely to document eligibility. If eligibility or permission
is uncertain, do not begin collection; refer privately to the owner.

Suggested assent check after approved guardian permission, where applicable:
"I understand what I would do, that I can skip or stop, and how to ask for my
study answers to be removed. I want to take part." A refusal or uncertainty ends
the invitation without recording study answers.

# Five-question assessment — pilot-v1

Frozen draft,2026-10-23. An unvalidated formative instrument, not an intelligence
test or proof of learning. Give the same questions before and after the session.
Choose one answer per question; "skip" is always allowed. A skipped/incomplete
assessment is not submitted or scored as a completed assessment. Do not enter
your name or free text. Collection remains disabled until the approved study opens.

## Q1 — Choosing an investment

The game's next training run is blocked by Data Quality. You have enough money
for one upgrade. What is the strongest next step based on that information?

A. Always buy more compute, regardless of the blocker.
B. Check and improve the listed Data Quality requirement, considering its cost.
C. Assume all resource upgrades have the same effect.
D. Wait for closed-window progress to remove the blocker.

## Q2 — Revenue and capacity

You enable a service and gain customers, but measured capacity shortfall rises.
Which statement best explains the tradeoff in this simulation?

A. More customers always mean every request is served and all revenue is earned.
B. High utilization alone proves that the player has failed.
C. Serving demand can compete for capacity; inspect fulfilled demand, shortfall
and training progress before choosing what to change.
D. Marketing creates free compute capacity.

## Q3 — Pausing and returning

You pause and confirm the run is saved before closing the browser for an hour.
What should happen when you return to the same browser profile and origin?

A. The saved run returns without an hour of added active progression; explicitly
resume when ready.
B. Every training run finishes automatically while the window is closed.
C. A new run always replaces the saved one without confirmation.
D. An online global leaderboard restores everyone else's latest run.

## Q4 — Future eras

What does completing GNT-7 establish?

A. That the player has built a real general-intelligence system.
B. That the game's dates and business returns are reliable forecasts.
C. That the player's Model Capability is a measured human IQ.
D. That the player completed a fictional scenario; future eras are speculative
and the game measure is not a real-world capability benchmark.

## Q5 — Research and simulation claims

A Journey card links a historical research paper and labels the game's mechanic
as simplified. How should that evidence be interpreted?

A. The paper validates every price, training time and revenue number in the game.
B. The source can support its bounded historical claim without validating the
game's invented tuning or proving a forecast.
C. A source link removes all uncertainty about future AI systems.
D. Every GNT era is exactly the real product with the same number.
